Speaker & Output

Output mode

Default is stereo: left/right cues split across two speakers. If your device only has one speaker, it automatically mixes both channels together — nothing is lost, you just don’t get the left/right separation.

Directional test

Detected output
Tap “Run the test” below.

Did the sound clearly move from one side to the other?

Why test? Phones and browsers can’t reliably tell whether your speaker is truly stereo. This quick check confirms it on your setup: real left/right guiding needs two speakers set apart. A single speaker still works well as a beacon your dog walks toward.

Share Commands

How to Use

  • Place the speaker where you want your dog to go — usually next to you. He learns to walk toward the sound.
  • Run the directional test (Speaker & Output) so you know whether your speaker can do left/right.
  • Come-here plays an attention chirp first (replaces your whistle) then a steady guide loop.
  • Train each sound. These tones don’t mean anything to a dog until you pair them with the action and a reward. The app just keeps every sound identical between you and your partner.
  • Playback holds a wake lock so it keeps going in your pocket.
Hearing safety: keep the volume moderate and don’t hold a very high-pitched tone close to your dog’s ears for long — canine hearing is far more sensitive than ours.

Hearing Check

This plays a series of pitches from low to high. For each one, watch your dog — ears perking, head turn, looking toward the sound all count as a reaction. It takes about a minute and you only need to do it once.

Do it in a quiet room, with the speaker you normally use, at a normal volume. If your dog is asleep or distracted, try again later.
